# LGPD Incident Runbook

1. Detect and open a timestamped incident record.
2. Contain access, rotate credentials and preserve evidence.
3. Determine affected tenants, data categories, subjects and risk.
4. Notify affected tenants immediately and support ANPD/subject notification within 72 hours when relevant risk exists.
5. Recover from verified backups, monitor, document root cause and prevention work.
